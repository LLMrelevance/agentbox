# Design taste

Their time is the bottleneck, and every round they spend saying "the spacing
is off" is a round this check could have saved. These are the corrections
they had to make more than once, counted across about 930 threads in
October 2026, most frequent first. Each count is a floor, because only the
latest reply on a thread is kept.

Go through the list against your own screenshots before you send them. Fix
what fails. If a rule and their request on this thread disagree, the request
wins.

## Presenting the work

1. **Many options that really differ** (21 threads). For a design or copy
   decision, show at least five that differ in concept, layout and placement,
   not five versions of one idea. "It's quite rare that the first design that's
   proposed is one that I'm a fan of." "These are all fundamentally the same
   thing."
2. **In the real window, in its states and themes** (15). Full window size,
   dark, in the states it will meet (an artifact open, full screen, zoomed
   out), and in more than one theme including the default, Ember. "Show me what
   it'd look like ... with an artifact open for each version."
3. **Look hard at your own result first** (9). Open every screenshot and frame
   yourself. Send only what is production-ready. "Before presenting them to me,
   I want you to review them and make sure they're polished."
4. **Label every picture, and check variants differ** (7). A short label and
   one line of explanation beside each, in order. "All these screenshots look
   exactly the same."
5. **Build exactly what was approved, and lose no feedback** (8). The shipped
   version is the approved picture, with every earlier note still applied.
   "I don't think that's the one I approved visually."
6. **When a round fails, restart from the problem** (7). Restate the problem
   and redesign; do not tweak the rejected set.
7. **A reference image is matched exactly first** (5), then varied.

## On screen

8. **Alignment and spacing to the pixel** (19, the most common complaint about
   a finished screen). Every element lines up with the text it belongs to, and
   gaps are even. They catch 4px. "It should be left-aligned with the text."
9. **Less on screen** (17). Remove every element, label and line that is not
   needed; new controls stay small and low-key. "This bar is a bit too
   cluttered now."
10. **It looks like this app** (14). Its own parts, sizing, type and
    translucent surfaces. "Can't evaluate these options since they're not
    actually following the UI/sizing of the real app."
11. **Square corners** (5), everywhere, including anything drawn to look like
    the app.
12. **Accent colour stays small** (10). Orange, yellow, purple or neon only on
    a dot, a key or a border, never on a whole button, headline, file name or
    count. "The orange is very overwhelming. I thought my app broke."
13. **No white or opaque blocks in a themed or dark window** (8). Surfaces stay
    the theme's colour or translucent.
14. **No stray hairlines** (6). A divider that separates nothing reads as a bug.
15. **One component style everywhere** (6). A redesigned pattern replaces the
    old one in every place that shows that thing.
16. **Readable in every theme, and in sunlight** (6). Check the contrast of
    text, shortcuts and links on the light themes too.

## Words on screen

17. **Cut what nobody reads** (12). No subheader, caption or second line that
    repeats the first; a secondary line fits on one line. "I never read the text
    below the header."
18. **Every header stands on its own** (10). Concrete words a newcomer gets; no
    clever fragments.
19. **Sounds like them, not like AI** (13). Plain full sentences; no em dashes,
    no colons in copy, nothing grand or markety. Build on their draft when they
    give one.
20. **Their exact words and capitals** (7), including the lowercase `agentbox`
    wordmark.
21. **Only true claims** (4). No absolutes, no implied features, no roadmap.

## Films

22. **Sound is crisp, never toy-like** (7): clean typing and clicks, music
    audible from the first second, no game sounds, no sudden volume jumps.
23. **Motion is smooth with one focus at a time** (7): no random zooms, no
    jump cuts, no bouncing to the beat.
24. **A talking-head cut is clean** (4): every slip and repeated sentence out,
    natural pauses kept. The first frame is never blank white.
